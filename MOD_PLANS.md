## Next planned review

**Batch 7 has not started.** There is currently no approved Batch 7 addition list ready to announce publicly. Candidates will be added here only after they have been identified and checked for:

- Minecraft 1.21.1 and NeoForge compatibility.
- Required dependencies and whether they are acceptable for this pack.
- Conflicts, duplicate features, and compatibility with Create / Aeronautics.
- Client-only versus dedicated-server requirements.
- Impact on existing world generation and the current world.

A mod being suggested by a player does not mean it has been approved or will be added.

## How players can suggest a mod

Use the [feature suggestion form](https://github.com/alexth110987/Create-IronWoven-Beta/issues/new?template=feature_suggestion.yml). Please include the exact mod name, its official project page, supported Minecraft / NeoForge versions, and why it fits Create IronWoven.

Suggestions are reviewed, not automatically accepted. The pack's existing compatibility and world-preservation decisions take priority over adding more mods.
