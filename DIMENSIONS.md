# Dimensions and Exploration

This page gives a short overview of dimension content. For the exact inventory, see [MODS.md](MODS.md). For planned additions and paused work, see [MOD_PLANS.md](MOD_PLANS.md). For test progress, see [TESTING_CHECKLIST.md](TESTING_CHECKLIST.md).

## Overworld

The main exploration and building area: biomes, terrain, caves, vegetation, resources, villages, structures, abandoned locations, Create-inspired engineering, transport, and automation.

The recorded world-generation baseline includes BYG 2.6.3 and Regions Unexplored 0.6.3. Existing worlds and their world-generation settings are protected during testing. Create Aeronautics Structures is an Overworld candidate ON HOLD and is not eligible for the next beta. Do not test or package it until an updated official CurseForge 1.21.1 NeoForge file has been reviewed and its known loot defects are verified fixed.

## Nether

Nether terrain, biomes, structures, mobs, resources, and exploration. No new Nether overhaul is currently approved.

## End

**Expansion is paused.** The long-term concept is an abandoned industrial civilisation with derelict factories, refineries, mines, settlements, broken railways, cargo infrastructure, and Create-inspired engineering. This is a future direction, not an active search or test queue. Historical End candidates are not approved additions.

## Shared content

Mods that affect more than one dimension are listed once in [MODS.md](MODS.md).

## World safety

World-generation and structure candidates must be tested in fresh disposable worlds. The production world and current BYG / Regions Unexplored setup must remain unchanged.
